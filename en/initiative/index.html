    Display

    Player reactions

    Players: they track their own reaction, so player cards have no Reaction button. Monsters keep the button. Surprised and Shield still work.

    Spells

    Rules and descriptions for spells: the Barkskin, Haste and Sleep presets (Sleep 2024 only), which spells need concentration, and which text the spell panel shows. Applies to spells only: the rest of the app (conditions, crits, concentration DC) stays 2014. Players with a D&D Beyond sheet follow the version on their sheet for each spell; monsters, players without a sheet and spells not on the sheet follow this setting. In the spell panel you can view the other version; that doesn't change the rules. Anything already running keeps the rules from when it was cast.

    Player view

    Players see the order, whose turn it is and player HP. Never stat blocks, notes or anything you hide (monsters via ⋯, the lair on its card).

    What just happened

    Status = Unhurt, Hurt, Bloodied, Near death. "What just happened": the last 2 lines (3 on a TV), only what the list also shows. Applies on this device, for all player views.

    On other devices

    Sharing with players didn't start. Reload the page.

    On this device

    A second window in this browser (TV via cable, second screen, tablet in split view). Works without internet too. On iPhone/iPad: open the site in Safari, not the app on your home screen.

    Help

    Explains the buttons, e.g. for a co-DM. During combat this shows the combat part, otherwise the prep part; the combat part follows when you tap Start combat.

    Storage

    Everything is stored on this device. Export now and then: as a backup, to move to another device, or to share your party with a co-DM.

    Or paste the text of an export

    For monsters outside the SRD. They're saved in this browser and then appear in the monster list like any other.

    Paste a stat block (e.g. from D&D Beyond): attacks, legendary actions, resistances and ability scores are recognized and empty fields above are filled in.

    Works with 5e-SRD-Monsters.json from the 5e-database by 5e-bits on GitHub (folder src/2014/en) and with an Open5e export. Your own JSON list in the same format works too. You can import several files one after another; they're merged.
