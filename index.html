Free tools for Dungeon Masters running D&D 5e at the table. Run the fight on your phone, let your players follow along and build your battle maps.

Open Initiative
  • Free
  • Works offline

Tools

Inn itiative

Encounter builder & combat tracker

Add SRD monsters and your party, roll initiative and track HP, conditions and turns.

Inn Sight

Battle map maker

Square or hex grids, your own map image, objects and painted terrain. Everything stays on your device.

What Initiative does

  • All SRD monsters, 2014 and 2024

    322 from SRD 5.1 and 331 from SRD 5.2.1, with stat blocks. Search, tap, done.

  • Your party in one tap

    Enter them once, by hand or from a D&D Beyond link.

  • Rules at hand

    Conditions explained. Concentration save prompted on damage.

  • Player view via QR code

    Put the turn order on the TV or your players’ phones.

  • Works on any device
  • No account needed

Your data stays on your own device.
